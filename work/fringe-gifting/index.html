Fringe Gifting Experience

Making digital gifts feel like real gifts

A focused interaction system that turned a routine points transfer into a moment of anticipation, celebration, and reveal.

Focus: Interaction design · Motion design · React · User testing

The challenge

Receiving points or a product should feel like receiving a gift, not like another account transaction. The experience needed to attract attention without interrupting the rest of the marketplace, then reward the user for opening it.

The interaction

A small navigation notification started the flow. Opening it expanded into a full celebration with confetti and a personal note. The gift-card form then flipped to reveal the reward and carry the user into redemption.

The outcome

User testing was strongly positive, and gifting became a central driver of how Fringe points moved across the platform. The interaction also established a pattern for later gifting and point-transfer experiences.
