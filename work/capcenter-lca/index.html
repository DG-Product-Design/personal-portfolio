Loan Officer Assignment Tool

Matching clients with loan consultants

I designed an internal tool that saved the company 750+ hours per year by assigning clients to loan consultants based on location, qualifications, and availability.

Project role
Product engineering lead + Frontend lead
Company
CapCenter
Time saved
Per manager: 30+ minutes/day (125 hrs/year)
Scope
I interviewed managers to find out why assigning clients took so long, then designed and built the tool that automated the work. I handled the interaction design and frontend implementation, including the controls managers used to review assignments and update consultant criteria.

Product-engineering contribution

  • Product engineering lead
  • UX + UI design lead
  • User research and A/B testing
  • Post-launch support
  • Design system
  • Component library

Technologies

  • Figma
  • React
  • Bootstrap
  • TypeScript
  • Azure

Key decisions

  1. 01Find the repetitive assignment work that was costing managers the most time.
  2. 02Turn location, qualifications, availability, and capacity into explicit matching rules.
  3. 03Give managers one place to review assignments and update the rules behind future matches.

Automate the assignment

Managers were losing time in two places: finding the right consultant for each client and keeping consultant criteria and capacity current. I built a tool that matched clients using location, skills, qualifications, and availability. Managers could review the results and update the underlying rules in the same place.

Give managers 125+ hours back

After rollout, the tool saved each manager more than 30 minutes per day, or over 125 hours per year. I designed the interface in Figma and built it with React, Bootstrap, and TypeScript. We deployed it through Azure CI/CD.
