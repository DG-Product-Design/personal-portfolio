The product context

The loan application sat at the center of CapCenter's digital business for mortgage, lending, and real estate customers. It was also one of the projects where my product design and frontend engineering responsibilities were most tightly connected.

The challenge

The government's URLA form is a complex document that must be filled out accurately to avoid legal and financial risks. It also contains a lot of optional fields that can be confusing for borrowers. We needed an experience that was easy for borrowers to understand with rigorous enough validation to avoid legal and financial risks.

What I owned

I led the user research and redesign, then built the frontend. That made me responsible for both what the application should do and how it actually behaved in the browser.

Results

In its first year, the redesigned loan application pipeline grew over 500% to $1.2 billion in loan volume. The redesign alone raised CapCenter's Net Promoter Score (NPS) by 2+ points.
