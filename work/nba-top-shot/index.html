NBA Top Shot / Dapper Labs

Personalizing NBA Top Shot messaging with collection data

I worked with Dapper Labs' data engineering team to make NBA Top Shot collection data usable in automated, personalized email for more than two million users.

Project role
Technical Consultant
Company
Dapper Labs
Timeline
September–December 2021
Scope
I worked with growth, data, and engineering on onboarding, personalization, and experiments for NBA Top Shot. I built the data connections and automation that made targeted campaigns and A/B tests possible.

Product-engineering contribution

  • Data normalization and sync
  • Marketing automation and A/B testing
  • Onboarding and campaign messaging
  • Internal tooling

Technologies

  • Customer.io
  • HubSpot
  • Google Apps Script
  • Slack

Key decisions

  1. 01Normalize collection data before sending it from internal systems to Customer.io and HubSpot.
  2. 02Represent ownership at the individual collectible level so campaigns can respond to what a user actually owns.
  3. 03Use clean collection data for onboarding experiments, A/B tests, and automated messages to more than two million users.
  1. SourceUser data from internal systems
  2. Normalize and syncPrepare data for Customer.io and HubSpot
  3. ApplySupport personalized email and onboarding experiments

Connect data to the experience

I worked with data engineering to sync internal databases with Customer.io and HubSpot. Before that data could drive personalized email, I normalized it into a shape the marketing tools could use reliably.

Support onboarding and internal operations

I supported onboarding campaigns and built internal communication tools with the Slack API and Google Apps Script.

Target messages down to individual collectibles

Once the collection data was clean, we could run A/B tests and automate messages based on the packs a user owned or an upcoming drop. We could target messages as narrowly as one collectible in a user's collection.
