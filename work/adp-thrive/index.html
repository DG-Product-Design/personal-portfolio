Choose the constraint that mattered

The ADP marketplace put Fringe in front of a new audience. The catch was account creation. Asking every buyer to register with Fringe added friction before they could purchase anything, so I made removing that requirement the center of the MVP.

Define and deliver the MVP

I scoped the first release around one complete purchase journey, not a collection of disconnected screens. I designed the experience and led the UI development, so decisions about scope and interaction behavior went straight into the shipped product.

Connect identity, commerce, and fulfillment

A React and Vite frontend used OAuth and an authentication proxy to call Fringe REST APIs. Stripe handled payment and fulfillment. Buyers stayed inside the branded Thrive experience without creating or navigating through a separate Fringe account.

Carry the product beyond launch

I stayed with the product through launch and supported it afterward. The simpler buying flow gave Fringe another sales channel and reached people who may not have found its products otherwise.
